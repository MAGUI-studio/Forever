# Imagens do Minimalista Moderno

Geradas com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens e salvas em WebP com qualidade 90. São artes decorativas do template; fotos cadastradas pelo casal têm prioridade. As imagens não retratam a identidade de Giovanna e Edson.

| --- | --- | --- |

## Prompts usados

### Capa

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-width wedding website hero photograph. Create a luxurious, understated wedding still life: two delicate yellow-gold wedding bands resting on ivory silk and a small dark stone pedestal, natural folds of satin, subtle warm sunlight, charcoal background with refined golden light. Landscape 3:2 composition, rings in the right half, generous dark negative space across left and lower areas for large white wedding names and countdown. Color photograph, beautifully realistic material texture, intimate editorial wedding mood, black white champagne gold palette. No text, no logos, no watermark, no people, no collage.

### História

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: portrait photograph for a modern wedding website story section. Close-up of the gently interlaced hands of an adult couple in elegant wedding attire, one in a black tailored suit and ivory cuff, one in flowing ivory silk, simple yellow-gold wedding bands. Faces entirely outside the frame, candid tender gesture, warm natural window light, soft neutral background, beautifully detailed natural skin and fabric. Vertical 4:5 composition, refined intimate wedding editorial, full natural colors with black ivory champagne gold accents. No text, no logos, no watermark, no collage.

### Dress code

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide wedding dress-code section background photo. Elegant couture wardrobe still life in a beautiful quiet dressing room: a black tuxedo with ivory shirt on a hanger on the far right, flowing ivory silk dress on the far left, champagne satin and subtle brass details, natural window light, rich black shadows and warm gold tones, editorial wedding photography, tactile natural fabrics. Landscape 3:2 composition with an uncluttered softly shaded center for overlaid text, atmospheric and sophisticated without excessive decoration. Full color. No people, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no collage.
